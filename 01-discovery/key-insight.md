# Key Insight

## Core Insight

Users do not simply want to act faster; they want greater confidence when making registration decisions.

## What This Means

The main challenge is not only the time required to register.

Students need:

- Timely and reliable information about class availability.
- A way to wait for a seat without repeatedly monitoring the Portal.
- Protection against losing an existing enrollment when attempting to switch classes.

## BA Implication

The solution should address the underlying decision-making and process issues rather than focusing solely on system speed.

This insight guided the definition of business requirements and the design of the proposed solution.
